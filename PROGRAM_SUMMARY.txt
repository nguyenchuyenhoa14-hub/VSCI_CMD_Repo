## Nature of the Problem
High-aspect-ratio Through-Silicon Via (TSV) interconnects require non-destructive geometric
metrology (TCD, BCD, Depth) from optical near-field signatures. This is an ill-posed inverse
scattering problem. Purely data-driven ML approaches degrade severely under out-of-distribution
geometric shifts due to the absence of physical electromagnetic constraints.

## Solution Method
Four-stage physics-informed pipeline:
  Stage 1 — TCD: RidgeCV (alpha∈{0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100}) on combined modal power + CWPC features
               (modal power: 5 modes × 65 WL; CWPC: upper-triangle phase matrix, variance-filtered).
               Physics: Poynting Modal Projection on 16 radial rings suppresses Yee-grid artifacts.
  Stage 2 — BCD: 150-component PLS latent space + KRR (polynomial kernel, degree=2, coef0=1)
               on depth-compensated log modal power with Cross-Modal Ratios (CMR).
               Hyperparameters via nested 3-fold CV grid search.
  Stage 3 — Depth: Deep Physical Integration (DPI) Phase Inversion.
              Fabry-Perot condition inverted analytically via Legendre-Gauss quadrature. 
              Zero free calibration parameters: boundary phase constant Gamma = -0.193 derived 
              from FDM eigensystem at nominal LUT config.
  LUT Generation: 1D FDM PML eigensolver for azimuthal modes m=0..4 at each (TCD, wavelength).

## Restrictions / Limitations
1. Validated within: TCD in [270,330] nm, BCD in [200,300] nm, Depth in [2700,3300] nm.
2. Boundary phase constant Gamma is grid-pitch-specific (1.64 nm Yee cell); requires re-evaluation if mesh changes.
3. Pipeline requires pre-computed FDTD CMD data; raw FDTD generation not included.
